The Invisible Threat

Every day, millions of seemingly innocent files are downloaded from the internet. Without isolation, a single rogue executable or disguised game installer can silently hijack your entire system, encrypt your personal files, and establish a permanent backdoor connection to hackers.

Unbreakable Isolation

Divte Vault forces untrusted applications into a secure, hardware-virtualized "Jail". The application believes it has full access to your PC and runs flawlessly, but any malicious code it attempts to deploy is completely trapped and permanently destroyed when the vault closes.

Total Identity Masking

True security extends beyond the filesystem. Divte Vault intercepts and scrambles your system's hardware identifiers (HWID) and completely masks your IP address from the isolated application. Even if an app tries to fingerprint you, all it sees is randomly generated fake data.
